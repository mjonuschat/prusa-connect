### Added
- [sdk] A driver describes each reading of a machine, such as the bed temperature, under /readings/<id>/ in the device's state. `connectorapi.Reading` writes a description, and `connectorapi.ReadReadings` reads the descriptions of one device.
- A Prusa printer and the echo machine now describe their bed under /readings/bed/. A CORE One and an XL with its enclosure on also describe the chamber under /readings/chamber/.
- A Klipper machine now describes its bed and each heater_generic and temperature_sensor section as a reading. The value of each section is written at /temperature/<id>, and the target of a heater at /temperature/<id>_target.
